Drill Team Prep

Designed for dancers interested in middle school, high school, or collegiate drill team auditions. Dancers focus on kicks, turns, jumps, flexibility, precision-based choreography, and performance confidence — with instruction from faculty who have real drill team experience.

What You'll Develop
Kicks & Jumps
High kicks, extensions, and explosive jump technique developed through progressive drills designed for competition-level height and control.
Turns & Technique
Clean, controlled turns with proper spotting and full-body alignment — the foundation of sharp, impressive drill team performance.
Precision & Lines
Sharp, uniform execution and synchronized movement for team-based performance. The hallmark of elite drill team training.
Flexibility & Stamina
Targeted stretching and conditioning specific to drill team requirements — full range of motion and the stamina to perform under pressure.
Performance Presence
Energy, confidence, and stage command developed through performance-focused training — because judges notice how you carry yourself.
Audition Readiness
Learn what judges look for and how to walk into any audition room with the confidence and preparation to succeed.
What to Expect in Class

  • Audition-style combinations that mirror the actual formats used at middle school, high school, and collegiate tryouts

  • Kick technique progressions built from foundational mechanics up to competition-level height and extension

  • Flexibility and conditioning work specific to drill team requirements — splits, oversplits, and functional range of motion

  • Precision-based choreography taught with the attention to detail and uniformity that judges reward

  • Faculty with real collegiate and professional drill team experience — not just theory, but lived knowledge of what it takes

  • Performance confidence training — presence, energy, and the mental preparation to stand out in a crowded audition room
