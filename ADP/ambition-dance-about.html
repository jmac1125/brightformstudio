About Us · Grapevine & Highland Village, TX

Built on Passion.
Driven by Purpose.

Ambition Dance Productions is a DFW-based dance education and production company offering technique classes, a competitive dance company, drill team training, professional choreography, and college & professional mentorship — for dancers of every age and level.

Our Mission

To provide dancers, singers, and actors of all ages an encouraging environment of technique classes, workshops, performances, and individualized guidance — where every student is given the opportunity to grow, excel their technical abilities, and build lasting confidence.

What We Stand For
Technical Excellence
Strong, safe foundations from day one. Every instructor brings college-level training or significant professional experience — so technique is never an afterthought.
Individual Growth
Every dancer is seen and valued. We nurture each student's unique artistry and confidence — meeting them where they are and guiding them to where they want to be.
Community
A family where dancers feel safe, supported, and challenged. The friendships, teamwork, and studio culture we build are just as important as what happens on stage.
Real Opportunity
Performance, competition, mentorship, and clear pathways to college and professional dance. We open doors — and help dancers walk through them ready.

Our Studios

Two Locations.
One Community.

We operate two purpose-built studios in the DFW area — Grapevine and Highland Village — so dancers across Southlake, Colleyville, Flower Mound, Lewisville, Keller, and beyond have a home close by. Every room is designed with the right floors, mirrors, and space for serious training.
