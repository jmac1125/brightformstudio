Arrival Guide
01
Enter at the Main Entrance

All dancers and families enter Grapevine High School at the main entrance (front office area). Do not use side or rear entrances.

02
Arrive Show-Ready

All dancers must arrive in full costume, hair, and makeup for both dress rehearsals and performances. No dressing at the venue except touch-ups.

03
Dressing Area — Library

Non-company dancers will use the school library as the designated dressing and waiting area. Company dancers will be directed separately by staff.
