Common Questions
Is the first class really free?
Yes — every new student is welcome to try their first class at no cost and with no commitment required. It's the best way to find the right fit for your dancer.
What age can my child start?
We offer classes starting at age 2 through our Young Children's Program. Our Ballet, Tap & Tumble class is designed for dancers ages 3–5, and Pre-Ballet welcomes dancers from age 3.
Does my child need prior dance experience?
Not at all. Many of our classes are open to all levels, and we have beginner-specific tracks for every age group. Our instructors meet dancers where they are.
How do I register for classes?
You can register online through our Studio Director portal. If you have questions about which class to choose, send us a message and we'll help you decide.
When are company auditions?
2026–27 auditions are scheduled for May 30–31 (Highland Village) and June 6–7 (Grapevine). Visit the Company page for full details and to email Wendy with questions.
Do you offer drill team prep workshops?
Yes — we run Sunday workshops (1–4 PM) at both our Grapevine and Highland Village studios, plus school-specific audition clinics for Emerald Belles, Fillies, Panteras, Marquettes, and Rosettes. See the Drill Team page for dates.
