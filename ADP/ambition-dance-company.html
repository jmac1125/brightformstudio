Philosophy, Mission & Vision
Mission

We are dedicated to cultivating strong, technically proficient dancers while nurturing their individual artistry and expression. Through expert instruction and a kind, supportive environment we empower every dancer to grow with purpose, perform with heart, and thrive as both artists and individuals.

Vision

A studio where technical excellence and artistic expression go hand in hand. We develop dancers who are not only strong and skilled but also confident, creative, and kind. Through a culture of ambition, integrity, and encouragement, we shape the next generation of artists and leaders — on stage and beyond.

Our Philosophy

Every dancer is seen, valued, and inspired to grow. We nurture dancers every step of the way — from their very first class to the highest levels of competitive performance. Each level encourages progress, technical development, artistry, and confidence while reinforcing teamwork, respect, and joy.

Strong technical foundations from day one
Opportunities to perform, compete, and grow at all levels
A clear dancer journey from beginner to pre-professional
A family community where dancers feel safe, supported, and challenged

Sparks Dance Company Where passion ignites
Ages 5–9
Sparks Dance

The beginning — where passion ignites one step at a time

Sparks is where the journey begins — a vibrant and welcoming space for young dancers to build skills, creativity, and love for dance. Age-appropriate technique, choreography, and costuming in a nurturing, high-energy environment. Classes emphasize rhythm, creativity, coordination, and technique.

Flicker
Every flame starts with a flicker · Ages 5–7

A team where tiny sparks of potential begin to shine. Flicker celebrates the joy of discovery, curiosity, and first flickers of passion for dance. Confidence growing, movement glowing, and learning to shine from within.

Weekly Classes: 2.75 hours — Tap, Jazz & Pre-Ballet
Summer: 10 hours & Summer Intensive
Flame
Fueling the fire · Ages 7–9

Flame represents the growing fire within each dancer — stronger, more confident, and bursting with vibrant energy that fuels focused skills and lights up every performance to shine brilliantly on stage.

Weekly Classes: Tap, Ballet, Jazz & Leaps & Turns
Rehearsals: Saturday weekly
Summer: 12 hours & Summer Intensive
Sparks Competition & Events

These groups attend 3–4 local competitions and 1 combined dance convention / competition per season.

Elevate Dance Company Where dancers rise
Ages 9+
Elevate Dance

Where dancers rise through technical training and artistic growth

Elevate is the heart of our training program. Here, dancers build strength, alignment, and versatility, preparing them for more advanced movement and meaningful performance. Classes emphasize clean lines, musicality, and detailed corrections — always delivered with support and encouragement. Growth, foundation, and rising together — building strong technique and artistry with heart and teamwork.

Ascend
Celebrates growth, resilience, and reaching new heights

Embodies determination and the excitement of pushing beyond limits, mastering skills and lifting each other up. Dancers are gaining strength, starting to rise. Every step is a climb towards confidence and brilliance.

Soar
Unleashing courage, daring to be bold

Encouraging dancers to push boundaries, express their unique style and rise with confidence and power. Gaining momentum in movement, technique, mindset, and artistry.

Radiate
Confidence and presence — every dancer shines

A confidence that comes from dedication and growth, inspiring dancers to illuminate the stage with their unique presence, emotional connection, and unstoppable energy. Poised, refining technique, and artistically bold.

Ambition All Stars Collegiate Dance Team Training
High School
All Stars Collegiate

Preparing dancers for collegiate dance success with enhanced training and performance

An advanced training program designed for high school dancers with their sights set on college dance teams. We cultivate the technique, performance presence, sideline strength, and audition readiness needed to shine at the highest levels of college dance (UDA/NDA style). This is not just prep — it's a launchpad. College preparation and mentoring for dance teams and dance majors is available starting as early as 9th grade.

01
Technique Elevation
Intensive focus on turns, jumps, advanced skills, and core strength. Building the technical foundation required for college-level performance.
02
Style Versatility
Training across multiple styles — jazz technique, pom, hip hop, contemporary. Guest choreography from current and former collegiate dancers. Exposure to different team aesthetics.
03
College Audition Prep
Mock auditions with feedback from college alumni and judges. Personalized audition reel planning and filming. Technique, etiquette, attire, and communication coaching.
04
Recruitment & College Coaching
1:1 strategy sessions, school research and guidance, recruitment timeline tracking starting freshman year. Parent information sessions and resources.
05
Game Day Readiness
Sideline choreography training with high energy and stamina. Pom technique and the performance presence required for collegiate game day environments.
06
Fitness & Conditioning
Injury prevention and collegiate-level endurance training. Building the physical foundation needed to sustain a demanding college dance team schedule.
